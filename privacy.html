Bungee Bonk – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Türkçe · Deutsch

English

Bungee Bonk is developed by Hece Games.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data ourselves.

The game saves your progress (best score, best stage, stars, unlocked skins, missions, daily challenge streak) and your settings (sound, music, screen shake, language) on your own device. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes: If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.

Contact: hecegames.app@gmail.com

Deutsch

Bungee Bonk wird von Hece Games entwickelt.

Wir selbst erheben, speichern oder teilen keine personenbezogenen Daten.

Kinder: Wir erheben wissentlich keine personenbezogenen Daten von Kindern.
